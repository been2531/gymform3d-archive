## 2. First 7-Day Gym Workout Curriculum (1 Episode Per Day)
- **Day 1 (Completed & Approved)**: **Cable Rope Triceps Pushdown** — Flared elbows & bent wrists (joint overload) vs. Pinned elbows & neutral wrists spreading the rope (100% lateral/long head triceps isolation).
- **Day 2**: **Dumbbell Lateral Raise** — Shrugging with upper traps & external rotation (shoulder impingement) vs. Scapular plane 30° forward lean & leading with elbows (pure lateral deltoid isolation).
- **Day 3**: **Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip)** — Pulling behind the neck / excessive momentum (rotator cuff shear) vs. Slight torso lean & driving elbows down to hips (latissimus dorsi full contraction).
- **Day 4**: **Seated Cable Row** — Rounded lumbar spine & bicep-dominant pulling (L4–L5 disc stress) vs. Neutral spine & scapular retraction driving elbows back (mid-back / rhomboids / lats).
- **Day 5**: **Incline Dumbbell Bench Press** — 90° flared elbows (anterior shoulder joint capsule strain) vs. 45° tucked elbow path (upper clavicular pectoralis major isolation).
- **Day 6**: **Romanian Deadlift (RDL)** — Rounded lower back & bar drifting away from shins (lumbar shear) vs. Hip hinge with locked lats & bar grazing shins (hamstrings & glutes full charge).
- **Day 7**: **Standing Barbell / Dumbbell Bicep Curl** — Swinging torso & anterior deltoid momentum (elbow tendon strain) vs. Pinned upper arm & supinated wrist curl (biceps brachii peak contraction).
