Your lateral raises are training your traps, not your side delts.

WRONG: Swinging the dumbbells up to 115° pinches the shoulder joint, and your traps shrug in to steal the work.

RIGHT: Shoulder blades down, arms about 30° forward in the scapular plane, stop at shoulder height and pause. Every rep goes into the lateral delts.
