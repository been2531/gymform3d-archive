Stop letting your shoulders and wrists steal your triceps gains on the Cable Rope Pushdown.

Here is the 3-step biomechanical checklist to isolate 98% of your Triceps Lateral Head:

1. TORSO & SCAPULA
✕ Wrong: Hunching your shoulders forward shifts up to 68% of the load onto your Anterior Deltoid.
✓ Fix: Set a slight athletic lean and depress your scapula down and back to lock the shoulder girdle.

2. ELBOW HINGE AXIS
✕ Wrong: Letting your elbows swing forward turns the pushdown into a mini pullover and drops triceps tension to 32%.
✓ Fix: Pin your upper arms (humerus) to your sides so your elbows act as a pure hinge axis (96% tension).

3. WRIST & LOCKOUT
✕ Wrong: Bending your wrists back at the bottom spikes wrist joint stress to 85% and cuts lockout short.
✓ Fix: Keep neutral wrists and spread the rope outward at the bottom for 98% peak Lateral Head contraction.
