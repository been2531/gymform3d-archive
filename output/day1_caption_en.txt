Stop turning your triceps pushdown into a wrist & shoulder injury. ❌➡️✅

1️⃣ ❌ WRONG: Leaning too far forward with flared elbows and bent wrists shifts the load straight into your wrist joints and front delts (20% triceps activation).
2️⃣ ✅ CORRECT: Lock your elbows tight against your ribs, keep your wrists neutral, and spread the rope apart at the bottom for 100% lateral & long head triceps isolation.
