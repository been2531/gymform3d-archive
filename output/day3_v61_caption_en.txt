Stop letting knee cave and forward lean wreck your Barbell Back Squat.

1. KNEE CAVE OVERLOAD (86% Joint Risk | 16° Knee Valgus)
Letting your knees collapse inward at the bottom of the squat twists your knee joint and places heavy shear stress on your ACL and medial meniscus.

2. LUMBAR SPINE STRAIN (79% Lumbar Load | 41° Forward Lean)
Pitching your chest forward turns the barbell back squat into a lower-back good morning, shifting compressive and shear forces onto your L4–L5 lumbar spine.

3. KNEE & JOINT LOCK (96% Joint Safe | 0° Knee Tracking)
Root your full foot into the floor, screw your feet outward, and lock your knees directly in line with your toes throughout the descent and drive.

4. HIP & QUAD DRIVE (98% Quad & Glute | 22° Hip Drive)
Brace your core 360°, keep a stable 22° torso angle, and drive hard through your quads and glutes out of the bottom for 4 sets of 8–10 controlled reps.
