Your squat breaks down in 2 places when it gets heavy.

Mistake 1: 16° knee cave. Knees collapsing inward put valgus shear stress on the inner knee.
Mistake 2: 41° forward lean. Tipping your torso forward moves the load off your legs and into your lower back.
The fix: screw your feet into the floor and track your knees over your toes. Brace hard and drive out of the bottom through your quads and glutes.
