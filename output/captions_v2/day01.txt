Your rope pushdowns are leaking triceps tension.

Mistake 1: +28° shoulder roll. Your shoulders roll forward and the front delts and traps steal the work.
Mistake 2: 35° elbow flare. Elbows drift up and down and the pushdown turns into a chest-and-lat press.
The fix: pin your elbows to your ribs, spread the rope 25° at the bottom, and squeeze a 1-second full lockout to hit all 3 triceps heads.
