Your Romanian deadlift should hit your hamstrings, not your lower back.

Mistake 1: 24° spine round. Bending at the waist instead of hinging puts heavy shear stress on your lumbar discs.
Mistake 2: 31° bar drift. Letting the bar float off your legs lengthens the lever arm and overloads your traps and spinal erectors.
The fix: brace, keep your spine flat at 0°, push your hips straight back, and slide the bar down your thighs with a soft knee bend until your hamstrings are fully stretched. Then drive your hips forward.
