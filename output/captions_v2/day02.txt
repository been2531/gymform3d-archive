Your lat pulldowns are training your arms, not your lats.

Mistake 1: 42° back swing. Leaning too far back turns a vertical pull into a lower-back momentum swing.
Mistake 2: +22° trap shrug. Shrugging and pulling with your hands lets your upper traps and biceps take over.
The fix: depress your shoulder blades first, keep a slight 15° thoracic arch, and drive your elbows straight down toward your hips.
