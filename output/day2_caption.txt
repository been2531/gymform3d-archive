Stop turning your Lat Pulldown into an upper-trap and biceps workout.

Here is the 3-step biomechanical checklist to isolate 97% of your Latissimus Dorsi:

1. SCAPULA INITIATION
✕ Wrong: Shrugging your shoulders toward your ears shifts 74% of the load onto your Upper Trapezius.
✓ Fix: Depress your shoulder blades (scapulae) down and back BEFORE bending your elbows (91% lat activation).

2. TORSO & BAR PATH
✕ Wrong: Swinging your torso excessively backward spikes lower-back shear to 82% and uses momentum.
✓ Fix: Lock a clean 15° thoracic arch and pull the bar straight down toward your upper chest and clavicle (95% tension).

3. ELBOW DRIVE ARC
✕ Wrong: Curling the bar inward with your wrists and forearms lets your biceps take over and drops lat isolation to 34%.
✓ Fix: Keep your forearms stacked vertically under the bar and drive your elbows down in an arc toward your hips for 97% peak Latissimus Dorsi EMG.
