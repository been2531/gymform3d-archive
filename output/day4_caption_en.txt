Romanian Deadlift — 4-Step 3D Biomechanics Breakdown:

• Step 1 (Side-Oblique View — Spine Round): Letting your lumbar spine flex under load (24° spine round) shifts up to 88% of the shear stress directly onto your lower back discs.
• Step 2 (Front-Oblique View — Bar Drift): Allowing the barbell to drift forward away from your shins increases the moment arm on your torso, overloading the erector spinae and upper traps (82% back strain).
• Step 3 (Side View — Spine Lock): Bracing your core with a 0° neutral spine and pushing your hips straight back turns the movement into a pure hip hinge (96% joint safety).
• Step 4 (Rear 3/4 View — Hip Hinge): Shaving the barbell down your thighs and shins locks tension directly into the biceps femoris, semitendinosus, and gluteus maximus (97% hamstring & glute activation).
