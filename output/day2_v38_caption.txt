Stop turning your Lat Pulldown into a lower-back row.

1. LUMBAR OVERLOAD (84% Risk | 42° Back Swing)
Jerking your torso backward turns the pulldown into a momentum row, shifting heavy shear stress straight into your L4–L5 lumbar spine and shoulder joints.

2. BICEPS & TRAP STEAL (74% Loss | +22° Trap Shrug)
Shrugging your shoulders at the top and pulling with your hands lets your upper traps and forearms hijack the tension before your lats ever engage.

3. UPPER LAT & TERES LOCK (96% Peak | 15° Scapula Lock)
Anchor your thighs under the pad, depress your shoulder blades first, and set a fixed 15° thoracic extension before bending your elbows.

4. FULL LAT ISOLATION (98% Activation | Elbows to Hips)
Drive your elbows down in a smooth arc toward your hips, squeeze your lats at the clavicle for 1 full second, and control the 3-second eccentric stretch. Aim for 4 sets of 12 reps.
